Privacy Policy

Effective 21 August 2026 · VOX for Even Realities G2 · com.ablaka.vox

VOX is self-hosted. You run the VOX server yourself, on infrastructure you control, using credentials you supply. The developer (Ablaka) and Even Realities do not receive, store, or have any means of reading your messages, voice audio, or contacts. There is no VOX-operated backend between you and the services you configure.

What is stored on the glasses

One value: the secret used to authenticate to your own VOX server, held in the Even Hub key-value store. Nothing else persists on the device — no messages, no audio, no contacts.

What is sent to your server

  • Voice audio captured while a recording screen is open (16 kHz PCM), sent over HTTPS.
  • The resulting transcription, the parsed intent, and the message you approve.
  • Inbound SMS bodies, delivered by a Twilio webhook you configured.
  • Inbound email, retrieved from the IMAP account you configured.
  • Contacts, templates, preferences and message history that you create.

This data is stored in a database on your server. Credentials for third-party services are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); the shared secret is stored only as an Argon2id hash.

Third parties your server contacts, using your credentials

  • Twilio — sending and receiving SMS.
  • Your email provider — SMTP to send, IMAP to receive.
  • OpenAI — Whisper speech-to-text.
  • Your chosen model provider — Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Ollama Cloud, for the tone rewrites.

Each of these is a direct relationship between you and that provider, under their terms and privacy policy. VOX passes your data to them on your instruction and holds no copy beyond your own database.

Microphone

The microphone opens only while a recording screen is on the display, and closes as soon as you stop the recording or VOX loses focus. Audio is streamed to your server for transcription and is not retained on the glasses. VOX does not listen in the background and has no wake word.

Retention and deletion

Your data lives on your server for as long as you keep it running. You can delete individual records from the dashboard, or delete the server and its database, which removes everything at once. Neither the developer nor Even Realities holds a copy to delete.

Children

VOX is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date, and noted in the app's release notes.
